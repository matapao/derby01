Changes made for this game: the textures are repacked into a paint base, a
paint mask and an emissive map; the texture file paths stored in the model and
the image metadata are blanked out. The source model geometry is unchanged;
the game scales and dents it at runtime and detaches its panels and wheels.
Additional cabin structure, engine details and crew are generated by the game.
