Paintball demolition derby. One player per car: sit inside, drive with WASD, aim with the mouse, left-click to fire the front paintball marker, and hold right-click to look back through the rear window and fire at pursuers.

Each car holds at most two players: the driver sits front left, and the other handles the turrets and engine — working the remote paintball markers on the hood (1) and the trunk lid (3) or jumping inside the car (2) to fix the engine and systems on the dash screens, in addition to cycling with C.

Every car is built its own way (stock, rally, drift, sport or muscle: pick it next to the car's row) and drives its own way; the add-on parts fly off when that side takes a beating. Nitro gives a burst of speed. Lamp posts, trees and bollards give way if you hit them hard enough; trees break into logs you can shove about.

The markers fire paintballs in an arc (the gun lobs them onto your crosshair) that splat over cars, the ground and the buildings. Paint on the windshield and the turret cameras blocks the view until the wipers clear it. The guns heat up while firing and a hot gun can jam: the gunner clears it by pressing the directions shown. Hits crack the windows, dent the panels and tear the rubber bumpers off.

The mechanic fixes what breaks in short games on the job screen, and puts out fires with the extinguisher (aim at the base of the flames, spray in bursts). Supply drops (fuel, spare tyres, bumpers, windscreens) turn up around the town: drive over one to grab it.

A radar spins on every roof and drives the radar screen in the corner. Shoot an enemy's radar off and their screen goes dark until their mechanic fixes it.
